Scope of Works
Development of a Secure Tenant Portal and Accommodation Management Website for Legacy Heights Student Accommodation
1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to develop a secure, web-based accommodation management platform for Legacy Heights Student Accommodation that enables prospective and existing students to conveniently apply for accommodation, select available rooms, submit documentation, make payments, sign lease agreements, and communicate with management online.
The platform shall be accessible via desktop, tablet, and mobile devices and shall comply with current cybersecurity and data protection standards.
2. Functional Requirements
A. Student Registration and User Management
The system shall provide:
Secure student account registration.
Email and mobile number verification.
Password reset and account recovery functionality.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) capability.
Role-based user access including:
Students
Accommodation Administration
Finance Team
Property Management Team
System Administrator
B. Room Selection and Booking Module
The platform shall provide:
Interactive display of available rooms.
Floor-by-floor room allocation view.
Room categorisation by:
Single room
Shared room
Premium room
Accessible room (if applicable)
Real-time occupancy status.
Room pricing display.
Online room reservation functionality.
Automated holding period during payment processing.
Waiting list functionality for fully occupied rooms.
C. Online Lease Application
The platform shall enable students to:
Complete accommodation application forms online.
Submit application details electronically.
Review tenancy conditions.
Accept terms and conditions.
Track application status.
D. KYC and Document Management
The website shall include a secure document upload facility allowing students to upload:
Mandatory KYC Documents
National ID / Passport
Student Registration Letter
Proof of Residence (where applicable)
Emergency Contact Information
Additional Documents
Sponsorship letters
Medical declaration forms
Other supporting documentation
Requirements:
Secure encrypted document storage.
File type validation.
Virus scanning of uploads.
Document version control.
Administrative approval workflow.
E. Online Payment Integration
The system shall integrate with Paynow Zimbabwe and support:
Accommodation deposits.
Rental payments.
Utility payments (if applicable).
Penalty charges and miscellaneous invoices.
Payment features shall include:
Secure Paynow integration.
Instant payment confirmation.
Automated receipt generation.
Downloadable payment receipts.
Transaction history.
Failed payment notifications.
Payment status tracking.
F. Proof of Payment Submission Module
The system shall enable students to:
Upload proof of payment after completing transactions.
Submit supporting payment references.
Track payment verification status.
Administrative users shall be able to:
Review proofs of payment.
Approve or reject submitted payments.
Generate payment reconciliation reports.
G. Digital Lease Execution
The platform shall provide a facility for:
Generation of lease agreements.
Electronic signing of lease documents.
Uploading of manually signed leases.
Secure storage of executed agreements.
Lease renewal requests.
Lease expiry notifications.
Students shall be able to:
View lease agreements online.
Download signed lease copies.
Track lease status.
H. Student Feedback and Service Requests Portal
The system shall provide a feedback management platform enabling students to:
Submit compliments.
Log complaints.
Report maintenance issues.
Request support services.
Features shall include:
Ticket generation.
Case tracking.
Response management.
Escalation workflow.
Automated notifications.
Management shall be able to:
Monitor response times.
Generate service-performance reports.
Track recurring issues.
I. Student Dashboard
Each student shall have access to a personalised dashboard showing:
Room information.
Lease status.
Outstanding balances.
Payment history.
Submitted documents.
Maintenance requests.
Announcements and notices.
Feedback status.
J. Administration Portal
The administration portal shall provide:
Occupancy Management
Room allocations.
Vacancies.
Check-in and check-out management.
Lease Management
Lease approvals.
Lease renewals.
Lease expiry tracking.
Financial Management
Invoicing.
Payment tracking.
Outstanding debtors.
Reporting
Occupancy reports.
Revenue reports.
Lease reports.
Maintenance reports.
Student feedback reports.
3. Security Requirements
The platform shall include:
SSL encryption for all website traffic.
Multi-factor authentication.
Role-based access controls.
Secure password policies.
Encrypted document storage.
Anti-malware protection.
Intrusion detection and monitoring.
Regular security auditing.
Daily automated backups.
Disaster recovery capability.
Audit trail for all user activities.

Email
SMS (optional)/Whatsapp
In-system alerts

Application updates.
Payment confirmations.
Lease approval notices.
Lease expiry reminders.
Maintenance ticket updates.
Feedback response notifications.

The website should be capable of integrating with:
Paynow Payment Gateway.
Accounting/ERP systems.
Student Information Systems.
Property Management Systems.
Email and SMS gateways.
6. Reporting and Analytics
Management dashboards shall include:
Occupancy levels.
Room utilisation.
Revenue collection.

Lease renewal rates.
Student complaint trends.
