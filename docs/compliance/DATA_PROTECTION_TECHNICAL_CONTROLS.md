# Data-protection technical controls

These controls support MIPF/Corelith's data-protection programme. They do not establish legal compliance, a regulatory registration or certification. Applicable Zimbabwean requirements, controller/processor duties, medical/identity lawful bases, signature validity and cross-border-transfer conditions require qualified review.

## Request handling

Verify the requester using the approved identity procedure and record request type, scope and received time. Use own-record exports or a narrowly authorized staff workflow. Review any active tenancy, service, financial/legal hold or immutable record before disposal. A privacy request's IN_REVIEW/ON_HOLD/DECLINED state is a review outcome, not evidence of irreversible erasure.

For eligible files, use the versioned retention policy, bounded dry run and independent approval process. The technical execution switch must stay disabled until the policy and operational deletion process are approved. Record completed/failed disposition and remaining legally retained categories. Correct factual errors through permitted updates or append-only corrections rather than silently rewriting signed or posted evidence.

## Production acceptance evidence

Maintain an approved data inventory/flow register, access roster, retention matrix, vendor register and incident contacts. Execute negative property/tenant/document access tests, credential/session revocation, export-secret checks, scanner outage/quarantine, hold/deletion concurrency and backup restoration. Record exact commands, revision, dates, failures/skips and scope limits in [TEST_EVIDENCE.md](../testing/TEST_EVIDENCE.md) and [SECURITY_TEST_RESULTS.md](../security/SECURITY_TEST_RESULTS.md).

User manuals/training material are supplied under `docs/USER_GUIDES`; a staffed training session, support hours and contractual maintenance ownership require separate handover acceptance.
